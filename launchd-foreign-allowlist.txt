# launchd-foreign-allowlist.txt — loaded jobs that are NOT ours to back up.
#
# Read by launchd-census.sh. One shell glob per line; text after # is the REASON and is
# required by convention, not by the parser — an entry without one is a future Claude's
# unanswerable question. Same shape as claude-blackbook/scripts/bb-writers-allowlist.json.
#
# WHY AN ALLOWLIST OF THE FOREIGN RATHER THAN A FILTER FOR THE FAMILIAR (acmeLedger-23,
# 2026-08-15). The census used to enumerate `com.braatz|com.strikezone` and call that "every
# loaded job". Those were the two namespaces that existed the day it was written. `com.user.ttyd`
# — loaded, KeepAlive, a writable shell on *:7681 — sat outside both for eight days while the
# census reported a clean bill of health for a set that did not contain it. A filter naming what
# we know fails OPEN on what we add next; an allowlist naming what is foreign fails CLOSED,
# because anything unrecognised lands in ESTATE and has to be backed or explicitly excused here.
#
# So: adding a job under a brand-new namespace makes this census go RED until someone says why.
# That is the intended cost. Do not add a `com.*` glob here to quiet it — back the plist instead.
